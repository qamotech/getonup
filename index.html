🔊 Pre-Alarm Volume Boost

About 1 minute before your alarm, GetOnUp plays a rising “volume check” tone that climbs to full, then your alarm fades in and plays at full in-app volume.

Honest truth: a web app cannot change your phone's system volume — no website can. So keep your media volume up and Do Not Disturb off (or allow GetOnUp). GetOnUp plays at full in-app volume, every time. 👍🏾

❓ How GetOnUp Works

Keep GetOnUp open (or recently opened) and your media volume UP. A web app cannot change your phone's system volume and can't ring reliably if the phone kills the browser completely — that's true for every web alarm. For an early shift, open GetOnUp at bedtime and leave it on the charger. 👍🏾

📲 Installing

Android: tap “Install on Android” and accept the prompt (or Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app).
iPhone: Safari → Share ⬆️ → “Add to Home Screen” → Add.

🔊 Sounds

Every sound is built live with layered WebAudio instruments — bells, bass, harmony and rhythm — no bland beeps, no robot voices.

💾 Your data

Alarms, settings, streaks and your sleep log live only in this phone's local storage. No accounts, nothing uploaded, nothing posted.
